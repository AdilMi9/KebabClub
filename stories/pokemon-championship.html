OPENING CEREMONY

The announcement came on a Tuesday — hand-delivered by pigeon, which everyone agreed was fitting. A scroll arrived at each gang member's door bearing the crest of Mustachestan (crossed mustaches, crowned) and the words, in Evran's unmistakable script:

ROYAL PROCLAMATION By order of King SuperWyndfal the Magnificent, the First Grand Mustachestan Pokémon Championship is hereby announced. All trainers and their companions are invited to compete. Venue: The Grand Arena of the Upper Lip. Date: This Saturday. Refreshments: Kebab. Presiding: Evran, Tournament Master, Kebab Master, Keeper of Secrets.
IGNITE"bro. BRO. I have been preparing for this my entire life. charizard is READY."

⚠ TOURNAMENT SUSPENDED ⚠
A Wild MEWTWO Appeared.

The sky above the Grand Arena went dark without warning. Then a light — purple, cold, ancient — descended from above. The stands went silent. Every Pokéball in every trainer's belt rattled simultaneously.

MEWTWO descended from the clouds with the slow, deliberate gravity of something that had decided to be here and did not require permission. It had been drawn by the power of the tournament. It had a challenge. It was not looking for a single champion.

It was looking for everyone.

IGNITE"okay so. so this is happening."
HYDROPHILIC"I want everyone to know that I am very hydrated right now and I think we're going to be okay."
IGNITE"hydro I love you and also this is not the moment—"
HYDROPHILIC"It is always the moment for hydration."
EVRAN"I anticipated this."
IGNITE"EVRAN DID YOU BOOK A MEWTWO FOR THE TOURNAMENT??"
EVRAN"I did not book the Mewtwo. I simply... did not exclude the possibility. These are different things."

SuperWyndfal stepped forward first, Alakazam beside him — its magnificent silver mustache gleaming in the purple light of Mewtwo's aura. It was, the crowd agreed quietly, the correct mustache for the moment.

Then everyone else stepped up. All at once. Even those who had lost their earlier rounds. Even those who had half their team fainted. Even Anduin, who was technically trying to befriend Mewtwo at the same time he was challenging it.

And from the shadows at the very edge of the arena, a figure appeared that nobody had noticed arrive: Burzum. He had not been at the earlier finals. He had not been visible in the stands. But he was here now, Absol at his side, because something needed him. That was always when Burzum appeared.

BURZUM"..."
BURZUM"...I'm in."

That was all he needed to say.

RAID BATTLE · ALL TRAINERS
Everyone vs. Mewtwo

Every Pokémon entered the field at once. The arena shook. Mewtwo's psychic aura expanded and then— paused, as if calculating. Twelve trainers. Twelve Pokémon. One very confused legendary.

Charizard and Blastoise attacked simultaneously — fire and water, Ignite and Hydrophilic side by side for the first time in a battle. The collision produced a roiling column of superheated steam that hit Mewtwo square in the chest.

IGNITE"bro did we just— did WE just do a combined move??"
HYDROPHILIC"I wasn't going to say anything but YES. I think that was a Steam Cannon."
IGNITE"we invented a move. WE INVENTED A MOVE."

Ana's Slowking made eye contact with Mewtwo — the first direct eye contact it had made with anything all tournament. Mewtwo stared back. Something psychic passed between them. Neither blinked for eleven seconds. The stadium was completely silent.

ANA (quietly, to Mewtwo)"you have very interesting pupils."

Mewtwo did not know how to process this. Slowking returned to looking at the pillar. It was processing something, clearly. It fired Psyshock three seconds later and Mewtwo staggered.

Jowisz's Gengar was found, somehow, inside Mewtwo's own shadow. It had apparently been there since Mewtwo arrived. How this was possible was a question for another time. Shadow Ball from inside the target's shadow was, geometrically, devastating.

JOWISZ"Gengar likes shadows. This one was very comfortable."

Burzum's Absol predicted every single one of Mewtwo's counterattacks. Not some of them. Every one. Each time Mewtwo moved, Absol was already positioned to redirect the hit. The other trainers began unconsciously following Absol's movements without realizing it. They were all, without knowing it, coordinated.

Lapi opened a sealed envelope from his inside pocket. It was labeled "MEWTWO CONTINGENCY (do not open unless Mewtwo arrives)." Inside: a twelve-point coordinated raid strategy, fully annotated. He had prepared this before the tournament began. He had told nobody.

LAPI (handing pages to everyone)"page three is yours, page seven is yours—"
EVRAN"I saw this envelope in his bag last week."
IGNITE"AND YOU DIDN'T SAY ANYTHING??"
EVRAN"I assumed it was handled. It was handled."

Then, in the hush after the final coordinated strike brought Mewtwo's aura to its lowest point — Evran's pigeon flew from his shoulder and crossed the field. The real pigeon. The non-Pokémon pigeon. It crossed 200 meters of psychic-charged air without difficulty, landed on Mewtwo's shoulder, and cooed once.

Mewtwo looked at it for a very long time.

Then Mewtwo left. Slowly, without a word, it rose back into the sky. The purple glow faded. The arena lights returned. Somewhere in the rafters, the scoreboard tried to display a winner and simply showed "????".

EVRAN"The pigeons always know."
IGNITE"...I'm not even going to ask."
EVRAN"Good."

CHAMPION: undefined
The Grand Mustachestan Pokémon Championship concluded without a declared champion, which Evran privately considered the most philosophically correct outcome.

Ignite demanded a rematch against Hydrophilic. Hydrophilic brought Blastoise a fresh bottle of water. Charizard accepted a drink from it, which Ignite publicly denied and privately did not stop.

Ana was awarded the Most Valuable Trainer trophy for her Slowking's performance. She was not present for the ceremony. She was staring at the trophy case. She noted, correctly, that the trophy case had structural issues.

Lapi's Metagross was voted most intimidating Pokémon in the arena. Lapi was not there to receive this recognition. The plaque was left on his desk. It had a small note beside it that said "thank you" in handwriting nobody recognized.

Violentine was contacted by three professional leagues. He did not reply. He was, presumably, leaning against something.

Rolex's Mew remains the most searched topic in Mustachestan's online spaces. Where did he get a Mew? He has not said. He's disappeared again. The countdown subreddit has adapted.

Burzum's Absol was the only Pokémon to go 5-for-5 on Mewtwo prediction. Burzum left after the battle without being photographed. This was expected. This was correct.

And Evran's pigeon — the real one, the non-Pokémon, the bird that has accompanied him through tournament battles and kebab consultations and pigeon court trials — received, by unanimous vote of the arena, an honorary Pokéball that it immediately escaped from and sat on top of.

The Pokéball remained on the ground.
The pigeon remained on top of it.
This was considered appropriate.
