& The Glorious Mustache
A tale of pride, heartbreak, and magnificent facial hair

Chapter I
The Most Magnificent Mustache in All the Realm
— ❧ —
In the grand city of Wyndfalia, there lived a hero unlike any other. SuperWyndfal was tall, brave, and capable of leaping entire city blocks in a single bound.

But his truest source of pride — the treasure above all treasures — was his mustache. Golden as autumn wheat, curled at both tips like twin crescent moons, it shimmered in sunlight and glowed softly in the dark.

Every morning, SuperWyndfal would stand before his mirror for precisely seventeen minutes, admiring its magnificent sweep. "You," he would whisper, "are absolutely perfect."
1
Chapter II
Carried Away by the Wicked Wind
— ❧ —
One dreadful Tuesday, the villain Baron Von Gustwick — a man with no mustache of his own and a great deal of jealousy about it — unleashed the Cyclone of Catastrophe upon Wyndfalia.

In the chaos, as SuperWyndfal flew bravely into the gale, an impossible gust swept clean across his upper lip. With a sound like a tiny violin, the mustache detached and was carried off into the swirling grey sky.

SuperWyndfal landed on a rooftop. He reached up. His lip was bare. Cold. Smooth. Wrong.

For the first time in his heroic life, SuperWyndfal sat down in the middle of a crisis and simply... stared into the distance.
2
Chapter III
A Hero Without His Mustache Is Half a Hero
— ❧ —
SuperWyndfal searched everywhere. He scoured rooftops and rain gutters. He interrogated pigeons. He hovered over the city for three nights, his bare lip cold in the wind, scanning every alley with his Wyndfal-Vision™.

Citizens whispered. "Is he... different?" children asked, pointing. He was. He felt hollow. He defeated Baron Von Gustwick that week, yes, but without flair. Without panache. Without the magnificent golden swoop that made his enemies tremble.

Winning had never felt so terribly mediocre.
3
Chapter IV
The Glorious Return
— ❧ —
On the seventh day, a small sparrow landed on SuperWyndfal's windowsill. In its beak — curled at both tips, golden and gleaming — was the mustache.

It had been found in a fountain in the Merchant's Quarter, gently resting in the water, perfectly preserved, still faintly glowing.

With trembling hands, SuperWyndfal pressed it back into place.

Click.

It fit like it had never left. A warmth spread across his face. The golden shimmer returned. Three nearby streetlamps brightened spontaneously. A dog three blocks away barked with what could only be described as approval.
4
Chapter V
Together Again — And More Magnificent Than Ever
— ❧ —
From that day forward, SuperWyndfal and his mustache were inseparable — literally. He had a tiny golden clasp installed, forged by the finest craftsdwarf in the Northern Mountains, just in case.

He leapt taller. He flew faster. He saved no fewer than eleven kittens that same afternoon, each one gently deposited back in a tree by his magnificent, gleaming upper lip.

And every morning, standing before the mirror for precisely seventeen minutes, SuperWyndfal would look at that golden, shimmering, utterly glorious mustache — and it would seem to shine back at him, as if to say:

"I never should have left. I love you too."
