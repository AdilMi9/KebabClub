01
Act One
The Word That Started It All

It began, as most catastrophes do, with a simple act of honesty.

Teberdar — known to his circle as Evran, Kebab Master of the First Order, Devourer of Döners, Keeper of Secrets — had been playing an online game when he encountered a fellow player of such spectacular incompetence that the words simply... escaped him.

EVRAN
"You absolute moron."

Three words. One ban. Seven days.

The ban screen glowed red on his monitor. Somewhere, a kebab went uneaten. A pigeon outside his window tilted its head, as if even it understood the gravity of the situation.

EVRAN
"...I stand by what I said. He was a moron."

But standing by it didn't unban him. He picked up his phone and texted the group.

02
Act Two
The Gang Assembles

Within the hour, the message had propagated through the group. The responses came in fast.

IGNITE
"bro they BANNED you?? those absolute pigeons. also hydro probably caused this somehow"
HYDROPHILIC
"why is it always me?? drink water, everyone, we need clear heads for this"
WHISP
"that's rough Evran, honestly though are we really doing a whole heist over this"
ANDUIN
"yeah let's go, I'm in, let's be friends with everyone there too"
ANA
"..."
EVRAN
"Ana, are you staring at a wall again?"
ANA
"the wall has secrets"

Evran laid a skewer of kebab on the table like a general placing a sword on a map. "The target," he said, "is Pigeon Entertainment HQ. They banned me. We unban me. Simple."

Everyone nodded. Even Ana, who was still technically looking at the wall.

03
Act Three
Inside the Pigeon Tower

The plan had many layers. Evran summarized them over a döner he ate for morale.

Lapi — quiet, competent, terrifyingly skilled — would handle the vents and the network access. Nobody asked how he knew about the ventilation layout. He simply did.

Ignite and Hydrophilic would create a distraction at the main entrance.

IGNITE
"easy. hydro, you flood the lobby"
HYDROPHILIC
"that's not what my powers are for—"
IGNITE
"retard, just make it slightly damp, they'll slip"
HYDROPHILIC
"...I mean I can do that"

Ana was assigned to monitor the server room. She found a wall in there and began staring at it almost immediately. Somehow, this was useful — the servers were unnerved and ran 12% slower.

ANA
"this wall has even more secrets than my wall at home"
04
Act Four
Maximum Chaos, Minimum Plan

The plan fell apart in the best possible way.

Hydrophilic had indeed made the lobby damp. Then a bit damper. Then the pipes started listening to him and the whole ground floor was ankle-deep in cheerfully hydrating water.

IGNITE
"BRO this is amazing actually"
HYDROPHILIC
"everyone stay hydrated!! this is fine!!"

Alarms went off. Pigeons scattered. Violentine found a wall to lean against and looked effortlessly composed while everyone else ran in circles around him. Whisp tried to keep spirits up — right up until the point Ignite started surfing on a document trolley.

WHISP
"okay I'm supportive of this mission but I did NOT sign up for this level of chaos"

Meanwhile Tista stood faithfully beside Evran as he typed furiously at the admin terminal. Evran, without looking up:

EVRAN
"Tista if I don't crack this in three minutes I'm biting your tits, just so you know"
TISTA
"...noted"
05
Act Five
Everyone Shows Up (Even the Rare Ones)

That's when the unexpected reinforcements arrived.

Jowisz appeared from around a corner nobody had noticed, producing a master access key from his pocket. No one knew how he'd gotten in. No one asked. He simply handed it over, nodded, and disappeared again into whatever dimension he usually occupied.

Bloodline23, rare as a solar eclipse, materialized in the hallway looking entirely calm and extremely useful. He blocked the corridor while Lapi worked the terminal.

And Rolex — famously elusive — had actually been there the whole time. Nobody had noticed. He'd been in the corner being funny to a confused pigeon executive.

Burzum showed up, acknowledged the situation with a single nod, contributed in some unclear but definitely real way, and then was simply... gone.

EVRAN
"Lapi."
LAPI
"done."

The screen turned green. The ban — all seven days of it — dissolved into nothing.

🥙 🐦 🚫➡️✅
MISSION ACCOMPLISHED
Evran logged back in that evening and ate a celebratory döner with three pigeons who had not been employed by Pigeon Entertainment and were therefore innocent bystanders.

Ignite sent Hydrophilic seventeen voice messages blaming him for the water damage. Hydrophilic responded by saying everyone should stay hydrated. Lapi was already asleep, having said nothing since "done." Whisp had recovered from the chaos and was being genuinely supportive again, in healthy doses. Ana was staring at a new wall.

Anduin tried to befriend two of the pigeon executives on the way out. One of them is now in the group chat.

Evran called the next enemy player a moron four minutes after his account was restored.
