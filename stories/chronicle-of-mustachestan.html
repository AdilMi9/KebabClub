Chapter IThe Chance Encounter of Two Mustaches

It was a Tuesday of no particular significance when SuperWyndfal, returning from a morning of modest heroics, noticed something on the corner of Merchant's Square.

A man was standing outside a haberdashery. He had a mustache. Not merely a mustache — a good one. Dark, curved, with evident intention. SuperWyndfal slowed his flight and alighted on the pavement for a closer look.

The man had noticed SuperWyndfal's mustache at the same moment. They stood for a long moment in mutual assessment.

THE STRANGER"That is an extraordinary mustache."
SUPERWYNDFAL"And yours is... very respectable."

The stranger understood the hierarchy instantly and completely. He was at peace with it — for he had lived his whole life knowing that his mustache was good, and that being the bearer of a good mustache was itself a gift.

THE STRANGER"My name is Reginald von Wyndfal."

SuperWyndfal blinked. "Wyndfal."

Chapter IIBlood, Destiny, and a Scroll of Parchment

The word hung between them in the afternoon air. Wyndfal. The same name. The same blood. They repaired immediately to the nearest tavern.

They were not alone. Seated at the corner table, already in possession of a döner, a pigeon, and a scroll was a Turkish gentleman of formidable mustache and considerable composure: Evran, sometimes called Teberdar, the Kebab Master of the First Order.

EVRAN"I knew you would come. The pigeons told me a Wyndfal had been spotted on Merchant's Square. There are things I know that others do not."
SUPERWYNDFAL"You know of Reginald?"
EVRAN"I know of Mustachestan."

He unrolled the scroll. On it: a map. Mountains. A valley. A kingdom whose name was written in illuminated letters of gold and vermilion. Mustachestan — the hidden realm where mustache quality determines rank, nobility, destiny, and the very succession of the throne.

REGINALD VON WYNDFAL"The throne has been empty for two years. Since the last king's mustache was lost in a wind incident."
SUPERWYNDFAL"I know something about wind incidents."
Chapter IIIThe Road to Mustachestan

They departed at dawn, three travelers and one pigeon. The route led through the Mountains of Chin, past the Valley of the Upper Lip, and across the Plains of the Philtrum — territories whose very names were drawn from the sacred geography of Mustachestan's founding mythology.

At the stream crossing, they found that Hydrophilic had, inexplicably, already arrived. He was standing in the water. He did not explain how he'd gotten ahead of them. He offered everyone a drink from his flask. Nobody refused. The water was, as always, excellent.

HYDROPHILIC"This valley has very good water. The mineral content suggests ancient volcanic geology. Also there's apparently a kingdom nearby?"
EVRAN"There has always been a kingdom nearby. The pigeons have known for years. As have I."

The journey took three days, which was fast by any measure, and which SuperWyndfal attributed to the fact that his mustache seemed to act as a kind of compass, tugging slightly in the direction of other magnificent mustaches, like a dowsing rod for greatness.

At the frontier of Mustachestan, they were stopped by two guards. The guards took one look at SuperWyndfal's mustache and stood back without a word. One of them appeared to be quietly weeping.

GUARD, COMPOSING HIMSELF"Proceed, sir. Please... please just go in."
EVRAN"I told you."
SUPERWYNDFAL"You did."
EVRAN"I always tell people. They rarely listen initially."
Chapter IVThe Grand Vizier, the Empty Throne, and a Suspicious Regent

The throne room of Mustachestan was vast, tapestried, and smelled faintly of wax and grooming oils. At its center stood an empty throne. Before the throne stood Grand Vizier Archibald the Wispy — ninety-four years old, bearer of a mustache that had once been legendary and was now a thin silver thread of historical significance.

When he saw SuperWyndfal's mustache, Archibald put a hand over his heart and said nothing for eleven seconds.

ARCHIBALD THE WISPY, GRAND VIZIER"At last. The Mustache has returned to Mustachestan."

From the left side of the hall, a figure stepped forward. Count Snarldor, Regent of Mustachestan, bearer of a mustache that was — and this had been suspected for some time, though never proven — glued on.

COUNT SNARLDOR"The throne of Mustachestan is not claimed by arrival. It is claimed by trial. I challenge this... newcomer to the Three Mustache Trials."

In the corner, the Royal Barber dropped his scissors.

Chapter VThe Three Trials, and The Fall of the Adhesive

The Three Mustache Trials were as follows: the Trial of Wind (withstanding a gale without flinching), the Trial of Gravity (holding a curl so perfect that a marble placed upon it would roll precisely to the tip), and the Trial of Recognition (being looked upon by the Royal Pigeon, who had never once been fooled).

SuperWyndfal completed all three with such ease that Archibald wept again, quietly, and did not apologize for it.

Snarldor attempted the Trial of Wind. The wind was not especially strong. But it was enough.

A corner lifted. Then another. By the time the gust had passed, Count Snarldor's mustache was hanging from one edge, trailing a visible line of dried adhesive, before detaching fully and drifting to the floor with the soft finality of a lie exposed.

ARCHIBALD THE WISPY"...I suspected this. For two years I suspected this."
EVRAN, FROM THE GALLERY"I suspected it for longer. I know things."

Snarldor was escorted out by the pigeon guards, who were both extremely efficient and deeply judgmental. The Royal Barber fainted for the second time that day.

Chapter VIThe Lady Maria, and the Recognition of Equals

Then the doors at the far end of the throne room opened, and Maria walked in.

She was the Duchess of the Eastern Provinces, daughter of the previous king's most trusted advisor, and heir to a mustache tradition stretching back eleven generations. She had been summoned, as Archibald explained, to attend the coronation as a matter of protocol.

She entered with the bearing of someone who had never doubted a single thing in her life, which was warranted, because her mustache was extraordinary.

Golden, perfectly curled, swept with architectural precision to two points that seemed to defy gravity through sheer confidence. It was neither superior nor inferior to SuperWyndfal's — it was simply equal in a different key. Like two great rivers that never merge, but recognize each other across a valley.

They looked at each other's mustaches for a long moment.

SUPERWYNDFAL"That is a magnificent mustache."
MARIA"So is yours."

Archibald began the betrothal paperwork before either of them had said another word. Some things, he explained to no one in particular, were simply obvious.

The Final ChapterThe Coronation, the Wedding, and the Happily Ever After

The coronation of SuperWyndfal, King of Mustachestan, took place three days later in the Grand Hall of the Upper Lip, before a congregation of twelve hundred citizens and approximately forty pigeons, all of whom Evran seemed to know personally.

Archibald placed the Crown of the Magnified Curl upon SuperWyndfal's head with trembling, reverent hands. The crown was ancient. It fit perfectly. It could not have fit anyone else — the crown, like all things in Mustachestan, was attuned to the mustache of its wearer.

ARCHIBALD THE WISPY, GRAND VIZIER"By the law of the Upper Lip, by the Covenant of the Curl, by the Sacred Clasp of the Golden Tip — I crown thee, SuperWyndfal, King of Mustachestan. Long may it grow."
ALL PRESENT"Long may it grow."

The wedding followed immediately afterward. Reginald served as Best Cousin, wearing his good-but-lesser mustache with the quiet dignity of a man who knows exactly where he stands and finds that standing perfectly acceptable. He was made Royal Cousin of the First Order, which was a title Archibald invented on the spot.

Hydrophilic produced a celebratory fountain from his palms that arced over the entire hall. Nobody had asked him to. Nobody objected.

EVRAN"I knew this would happen. The pigeons knew years ago. I tried to tell people. Nobody listens until it is already true."

SuperWyndfal and Maria stood before the altar. They looked at each other. Then at each other's mustaches. Then at each other again. They understood each other completely and without ambiguity.

The Royal Barber, now recovered, wept so profusely during the ceremony that Hydrophilic had to quietly remove him before the floor became a concern.

Happily Ever After
SuperWyndfal and Maria ruled Mustachestan with equal wisdom and equal mustaches for many years thereafter. The kingdom flourished. The mustache tradition was preserved, codified, and expanded into a twelve-volume encyclopedia authored primarily by Evran, who had been waiting for someone to ask him to write it for years and who dedicated it to his pigeons.

Reginald served faithfully as Royal Cousin of the First Order. He tended his good-but-lesser mustache with meticulous care, and was respected by all for the grace with which he occupied his exact place in the hierarchy of mustaches.

Archibald the Wispy retired at the age of ninety-seven with the title Grand Vizier Emeritus. His silver-thread mustache was preserved in the Royal Museum, behind glass, with a small plaque reading: "It was once magnificent. It remembers."

Hydrophilic was appointed Royal Hydrologist of Mustachestan, a position created specifically so that someone would stop him producing unauthorized indoor fountains. It worked approximately forty percent of the time.

The Royal Barber fainted three more times in the first year of the reign, each time upon seeing the King and Queen's mustaches in the morning light. He considered this a privilege.
